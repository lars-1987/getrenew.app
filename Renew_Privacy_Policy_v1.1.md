# Privacy Policy

**Renew — Skincare Tracking App**

*Last updated: February 2026*

## Overview

Renew ("we", "our", "us") is operated by RenewPro, a sole trader registered in Australia. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Renew mobile application ("the App").

**By using Renew, you agree to this Privacy Policy.** If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

| Data Type | Purpose | Required |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Email address** | Account creation, login, password reset | Yes |
| **Name** (optional) | Personalisation within the App | No |
| **Product information** | Skincare products you add to your library | No |
| **Routine data** | Routines you create and daily tracking | No |
| **Photos** | Product photos and profile photos you upload | No |
| **Location** (approximate) | UV index feature (city-level only) | No |

### How Your Photos Are Stored

Your photo storage depends on your subscription tier:

| Subscription | Storage Location | Sync Across Devices |
|--------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Free** | Your device only (local storage) | No |
| **Pro** | Your device + secure cloud storage | Yes |

**Free users:** Photos are stored only on your device. If you delete the app or switch devices, your photos will not be available on the new device.

**Pro users:** Photos are automatically uploaded to secure cloud storage (Supabase) and can sync across all your devices. When you upgrade to Pro, any existing photos on your device will be uploaded to the cloud.

Photos are compressed before storage to optimise performance and storage space.

### Information Collected Automatically

| Data Type | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Device information** | App functionality, troubleshooting |
| **App usage data** | Improving the App, understanding feature usage |
| **Crash reports** | Fixing bugs and improving stability |

### Skin Journal Photos

Renew includes an optional photo journal feature for tracking your skin over time. If you choose to use this feature:

| Aspect | How We Handle It |
|--------|------------------|
| **What you upload** | Photos of your face/skin that you choose to take |
| **Storage** | Same as product photos (Free: local only, Pro: local + cloud) |
| **Access** | Only you can see your journal photos |
| **AI processing** | Journal photos are NOT sent to any AI service |
| **Biometric data** | We do not extract or store biometric identifiers from your photos |
| **Deletion** | You can delete any journal photo at any time |

**Important:** Skin journal photos are entirely optional. The app functions fully without them.

### AI-Powered Features

Renew uses artificial intelligence to help you add products more easily:

| Feature | How It Works | Data Sent |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| **Ingredient extraction from URLs** | When you paste a product URL, we may fetch the page and use AI to extract product details | The product webpage content (not your personal data) |
| **Product photo analysis** | When you photograph a product, AI can identify the product name, brand, and ingredients | The product photo only (not journal photos) |

**AI Service Provider:** We use Google Gemini API for AI processing.

**What we send:** Only product-related content (URLs, product photos). We never send your personal information, journal photos, or skin photos to AI services.

**What we don't do:**
- ❌ Use AI to analyse your skin or face
- ❌ Store AI-processed data beyond what you save to your profile
- ❌ Use your data to train AI models
- ❌ Share your product data with third parties for AI training

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ Health or medical diagnoses
- ❌ Biometric identifiers (facial recognition data, fingerprints)
- ❌ Precise GPS location
- ❌ Contacts or address book
- ❌ Payment card details (handled by Apple/Google)

## Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Account data** | Until you delete your account |
| **Product & routine data** | Until you delete your account |
| **Product photos (Free users)** | Stored on your device only; we do not have access |
| **Product photos (Pro users)** | Until you delete them or your account |
| **Journal photos (Free users)** | Stored on your device only; we do not have access |
| **Journal photos (Pro users)** | Until you delete them or your account |

When you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- **Access** — Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction** — Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion** — Request deletion of your data
- **Portability** — Receive your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** — Where processing is based on consent
- **Complaint** — Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

### For Australian Users

You have rights under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. You can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au) if you have concerns.

### For EU/UK Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our lawful bases for processing are:

- **Contract** — To provide the App services you requested
- **Legitimate interests** — To improve and secure the App
- **Consent** — For optional features like marketing emails (if applicable)

### For California Users (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not sell personal information.
